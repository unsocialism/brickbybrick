    Reading settings

    Paragraph shape
    Sentences per block2
    Leave paragraphs alone up to3 sent.

    Short paragraphs and dialogue stay as the author wrote them; only longer ones get broken up.

    Break blocks longer than35 words

    A single very long sentence is split at commas, dashes and semicolons so it never becomes a block of its own.

    Colour
    Contrast80%

    Lower contrast usually calms the static. Pure black on pure white is the worst case.

    Highlight strength35%

    Each block gets its own tinted background, so the page colour separates the blocks. By default the tint follows the theme.
